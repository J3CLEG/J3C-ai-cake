# Weekly briefing, 2026-11-15

*Education only, not investment advice. This branch builds on `weekly/2026-11-08` (PR #4), and PRs #1 to #4 are still unmerged. Merging this pull request brings in all five weeks.*

## The week in one paragraph
The numbers got stronger and the people risk got bigger. CoreWeave beat and raised on Nov 10 (revenue $3.66B, +168%; 2026 capex floor raised to $37B), and Anthropic's amended S-1 showed $33.2B of revenue in nine months and an ~$81B run-rate. Then NVIDIA's board accepted Jensen Huang's resignation on Nov 11, with CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO (8-K). Export controls look set to tighten. Credit relaxed (high-yield spread 3.20%, from 3.48%) but the 10-year Treasury jumped to 5.10%. No big-four capex change. Stage stays late build-out. Next tests: NVIDIA results (reported for Nov 17) and Anthropic pricing (reported for Nov 18).

## By layer
- **Chips:** Huang resigned as CEO and director, effective Nov 11; Harvey Jones is chair (8-K, T1). Reports say the stock fell about 4% on Nov 9 and again on Nov 12, and several brokers cut targets. The Information reports the White House may not block new NDAA chip controls, and CBS reports growing support in Congress. No rule has been issued (contested).
- **Infrastructure:** CoreWeave Q3: backlog about $132.5B, 2026 revenue guide $13.0–13.3B, capex $37–39B (was $35–39B) (T1). Nebius Q3 revenue $980.8M, up 571% (6-K, T1).
- **Models:** Anthropic S-1/A No. 2 (Nov 10): 825M shares at $70–78 on Nasdaq as ANT, plus a $26B Class N placement to Google, NVIDIA, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft and TSMC (T1). Anthropic also cut Fable 5.2 prices about 20%, to $8/$40 per million tokens (two newsletters agree).
- **Energy:** Duke Energy reached a North Carolina settlement with Amazon, Google, Meta, Microsoft and others. New loads of 50 MW or more pay upfront for dedicated grid upgrades. It still needs utility commission approval. Morgan Stanley reportedly sees about 20% of 2028 US data center additions slipping to 2029 (single source, contested).
- **Capital:** High-yield spreads eased to 3.20% while the 10-year rose 26bp, so all-in junk borrowing costs barely moved. CoreWeave says it will not borrow unsecured "at current yields". It is leaning on about $9B of customer prepayments instead.

## Money in vs money out
Latest quarterly capex (SEC, T1) is unchanged: Amazon $77.3B, Alphabet $51.4B, Microsoft $39.4B, Meta $33.9B (September quarter) and Oracle $28.5B (August quarter), about **$230B a quarter**. Revenue signals strengthened: Anthropic's ~$81B run-rate, CoreWeave +168%, Nebius +571%, AWS +39%, Azure +48%. Revenue is growing fast but is still a fraction of capex. CoreWeave's $846M quarterly interest bill against $207M operating income shows how much of the build-out runs on borrowed money.

## Cycle clock
**Stage: Build-out. Position stays 0.90.** Demand data improved and nobody cut capex. The CEO exit is a risk to watch, not a stage change.
- **10-year Treasury:** **watch → warn** at 5.10% (from 4.84%). A 26bp jump in a week raises the hurdle rate for every leveraged project.
- **High-yield spreads:** stays **watch** at 3.20% (from 3.48%).
- **Neocloud leverage:** stays **bad**. Results were strong, but CoreWeave is shut out of unsecured debt at acceptable yields.
- **AI revenue vs capex:** stays **watch**, with a stronger value (Anthropic filing).
- **Capex revisions, FCF, debt issuance:** unchanged.

The bottleneck stays **energy**.
