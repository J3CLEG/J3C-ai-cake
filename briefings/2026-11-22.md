## The week in one paragraph
Both big tests passed, and the market sold off anyway. NVIDIA beat on Nov 17 (8-K, T1): revenue was $115.8B (+103%), and it guided Q4 to $130B. It also guided gross margin down about 3 points and assumes no China data center revenue. Anthropic priced the largest IPO on record at $83, above its range, raising about $81.3B. ANT opened at $111.50 and closed its first day at $97.17 (+17%). Under the deals, credit got tighter. The high-yield spread jumped to 3.52% from 3.20%, stocks fell six sessions in a row, and new US chip rules pushed TSMC to halt some China orders. Capex did not change. The stage stays late build-out, and the position moves up a notch.

## By layer
- **Chips:** NVIDIA Q3 FY27: Data Center revenue $107.6B. Non-GAAP gross margin was 74.9%, against 75.0% last quarter. Q4 guide is $130B ±3% at a 71–72% margin. $227B of buyback authorization remains (T1). Wire reports say new US rules ended China's remote access to chips and led TSMC to halt China orders at 16nm and below without licences. Treasury Secretary Bessent says no further limits are needed (contested; no rule text read).
- **Infrastructure:** Blackstone's new REIT agreed to buy 75% of two AWS joint ventures (13 powered shells) for about $5B at a 7.0% cap rate. AWS leases them back for 12 years, extendable to 32 (8-K, T1). That is a hyperscaler recycling building capital into chips.
- **Energy:** Transmission owners asked FERC (around Nov 16) to approve 10–20 year contracts for big loads with collateral. In PJM, a load of 50 MW or more would commit to at least 80% of the service it requests for at least 10 years (Latitude, MGRID). Latitude Intelligence counts about 120 GW of signed data center contracts against 537 GW of reported pipeline (22%). That figure comes from one source, so it is not used in the data.
- **Models:** Anthropic IPO: 980M shares at $83, about $81.3B gross (StockTitan, Admirals, Forbes). That values it at about $1.1T on basic shares, or about $1.5T fully diluted. OpenAI's round is still only reported, as a ~$11B first close at $1.25T against a target cut to $20B. That is not a down round.
- **Capital:** CoreWeave fell about 21% from Nov 12 to Nov 19. Co-founder Venturo says he bought $30M of the 2031 notes. Its unsecured yields were reported at more than 16–17% earlier in the month.

## Money in vs money out
Latest quarterly capex (SEC, T1) is unchanged at about **$230B**: Amazon $77.3B, Alphabet $51.4B, Microsoft $39.4B, Meta $33.9B and Oracle $28.5B. Money out keeps landing at NVIDIA, with $107.6B of Data Center revenue in one quarter and $43.8B of free cash flow. Money in downstream is growing but still much smaller: Anthropic has a ~$81B run-rate, and AWS and Azure grew 39% and 48%. Public markets now fund the model layer directly: Anthropic's $78.8B of primary proceeds equals about a third of one quarter of big-five capex.
