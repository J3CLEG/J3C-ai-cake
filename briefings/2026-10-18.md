# Weekly briefing, 2026-10-18

*Education only, not investment advice. First weekly run since the site was seeded on 2026-10-15.*

## The week in one paragraph
The supply side of the cake kept getting stronger while the money side started to crack at the weakest point. TSMC posted a record quarter and guided Q4 revenue up as much as about 61% year over year. ASML said its planned supply for 2027 and 2028 will not fully meet demand. Then a hot CPI print (with the Fed already having hiked in September, per Dow Jones) hit the most leveraged AI borrower. CoreWeave's 9.75% unsecured notes fell to about 81 cents, yielding over 15%, and its default-swap cost jumped. Goldman said AI-linked high-yield spreads widened about 25bp in a day while non-AI spreads barely moved. The broad high-yield index is still calm at 3.23% (FRED), so for now this is a single-name and CCC-tier repricing, not a credit-wide event.

## By layer
- **Energy:** Google signed a 20-year PPA with Constellation for 890 MW of nuclear uprates at 11 existing units (about $4.3B of Constellation investment), plus a separate 15-year supply deal for 2,700 MW (Oct 6). Oracle agreed to take 10 to 20% of Point Beach nuclear output via We Energies for its Port Washington campus with OpenAI. This still needs state approval.
- **Chips:** TSMC Q3 revenue was $46.7B (+41% in dollars) at a 66.4% gross margin, with Q4 guided to $52.3B to $54.3B (T1). ASML had record Q3 sales of EUR 11.7B and says supply will fall short of 2027 and 2028 demand (T1). IDC, via Reuters, puts NVIDIA's share of China AI chips at 34% in H1 2026, against Huawei's 28%. That rests on one original source and is not added to data.
- **Infrastructure:** Capex rows were upgraded to company sources. Amazon is at about $220B (Jassy, Q2 call). Meta is at $130B to $145B (Q2 release). Alphabet is at $195B to $205B (CFO on Q2 call, via Tier 2). Microsoft is unchanged at about $190B and not re-verified: its call said only that FY27 capex will grow.
- **Models:** Anthropic reportedly shared Q3 financials with investors ahead of a possible November IPO at about $2T. OpenAI is reportedly in talks to raise $30B at about $1.4T. Both are press reports only and sit in the contested panel.
- **Capital:** CoreWeave's bonds trade like distressed debt (S&P B category; one analyst says "trading like CCC"). Its five-year CDS rose to about 881bp from about 547bp at end-June. Nebius fell more than 10% in sympathy, though its funding is mostly converts, secured loans and customer prepayments. CCC spreads are at 12.67 points, against 9.04 in May.

## Money in vs money out
Latest actual quarterly capex (SEC, Tier 1): Amazon $54.2B, Alphabet $44.9B, Microsoft $35.8B, Meta $30.1B (all June quarter), Oracle $28.5B (August quarter). That is about **$194B in one quarter**. On the revenue side, AWS made $42.2B in Q2 (+36.7%), and the backlogs are Amazon $496B and Google Cloud $514B. Backlog says demand is real. But Meta's Q2 free cash flow was only **$0.78B against $31.1B of capex** (T1), so the gap is now funded by debt, leases and partners.

## Cycle clock
**Stage: Build-out. Position 0.80 → 0.85.** Reason: credit markets have started pricing the marginal AI borrower as distressed while hyperscaler capex is still rising. That is a late-build-out trait. Signal changes:
- **Neocloud leverage:** warn → **bad**. CoreWeave's unsecured yield is above 15%.
- **Capex guidance:** stays build, now on Tier 1.
- **Hyperscaler FCF:** stays warn, now on Tier 1 (Meta's $0.8B).
- **High-yield spreads:** pending → **watch** at 3.23%. The index is calm but CCC and AI-specific spreads are wide.
- **New signal:** the 10-year Treasury yield is at 5.10% (watch).

The bottleneck stays **energy**. ASML's warning hints that lithography could join it in 2027 and 2028.

## Exit rules tripped
- **Infrastructure, "Neocloud refinancing at sharply wider spreads": near-trip, not tripped.** Secondary yields are above 15%, but CoreWeave has not refinanced at those levels yet. Any new unsecured issue would trip it.
- No other rule tripped. No capex cuts, no utility load-forecast cuts, and no lab down rounds. NVIDIA gross margin, GPU rental prices and application NRR were not tested this week.

- Anthropic IPO: about $2T target and a November listing, against a $1.35T last private mark. There is no public S-1 yet.
- OpenAI: talks on $30B at about $1.4T, from a single report.
