## The week in one paragraph
Markets repriced the AI trade hard while the underlying numbers held. AP reported the S&P 500 fell 4.4% on Nov 5, its worst day since the April 2025 tariff shock, and the Nasdaq Composite fell 5.5%. Credit moved too: the high-yield spread rose to **3.48%** from 3.26% (FRED, Nov 5), and Reuters reported CoreWeave's CDS past 1,000bp to record highs. No company cut capex this week.
