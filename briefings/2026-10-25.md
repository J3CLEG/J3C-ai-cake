# Weekly briefing, 2026-10-25

*Education only, not investment advice. This branch builds on last week's `weekly/2026-10-18`, which is not merged yet. Merging this pull request brings in both weeks.*

## The week in one paragraph
Meta opened hyperscaler earnings season and showed the core tension of the cycle in one release. Revenue rose 26% to $64.6B, and Meta raised the low end of its 2026 capex guide to $135–145B. But free cash flow was **negative $2.1B**: $32.8B of operating cash flow did not cover $34.9B of capex including finance leases (Tier 1). Credit stress at the weakest borrowers got worse. CoreWeave's 2031 notes hit a record yield of about 15.8%, and its stock is about 70% below its peak. The FT reported that AI data center project bonds are trading below par. The broad high-yield index widened to 3.40% from 3.23% but is still calm. In private markets, OpenAI reportedly lowered its funding ask to $1.25T from $1.4T. The WSJ reported that Anthropic's draft prospectus shows $16.3B of first-half revenue and an $80.7B net loss, about $72B of it non-cash. Alphabet, Microsoft and Amazon report this coming week, and Amazon is confirmed for Oct 29.

## By layer
- **Chips:** Reports from Seoul Economic Daily, relayed by Techzine and TNW, say Nvidia wants Samsung, SK hynix and Micron to build the NVHBM base die on TSMC's 3nm process. That would put memory in the same queue as GPUs for TSMC's leading-edge lines. UBS sees 2027 HBM demand of about 61.5B Gb against about 54.6B Gb of supply. That is one broker estimate, so it sits in the contested panel.
- **Infrastructure:** Meta's 2026 capex is now $135–145B (T1). For 2027, Meta says only that it will "continue investing aggressively" and gives no number yet.
- **Models:** OpenAI is reportedly raising up to $30B at $1.25T pre-money, led by Coatue and UAE investors (CNBC via TechCrunch). That is lower than the $1.4T ask but still above its $852B mark from March, so it is **not a down round**. Anthropic's public S-1 was expected as soon as Oct 23, but none was found on EDGAR at this run. The draft figures all trace back to one WSJ review, so they stay contested.
- **Capital:** CoreWeave's 2031 notes reached a record 15.76% yield and its CDS was reported at 1,115bp (Benzinga, Oct 22). HSBC repeated its Reduce rating. Nebius and IREN fell in sympathy. The FT says project bonds for AI data centers are trading below par, which is the first sign that stress is spreading beyond CoreWeave (single source, contested).
- **Energy, applications:** Quiet. No verified new PPAs or load-forecast changes this week.

## Money in vs money out
Latest actual quarterly capex (SEC, Tier 1): Amazon $54.2B, Alphabet $44.9B and Microsoft $35.8B (all June quarter); **Meta $33.9B (September quarter, new)**; Oracle $28.5B (August quarter). That totals about **$197B per quarter**. Revenue signals: Meta grew 26% and guides Q4 to $73–76B, so ad demand is paying for part of the build. But its cash flow no longer covers the build. Anthropic's reported $16.3B of first-half revenue (single source) sits against up to $750B of compute commitments. That is the lab-level version of the same gap.

## Cycle clock
**Stage: Build-out. Position 0.85 → 0.88.** Reason: a big-four hyperscaler now spends more than its operations generate while still raising guidance, and credit stress widened from one neocloud toward AI project bonds. Signal changes:
- **Hyperscaler free cash flow:** warn → **bad**. Meta's Q3 FCF was −$2.1B (T1).
- **Capex guidance:** stays **build**, because Meta raised its floor.
- **Neocloud leverage:** stays **bad**. Yields hit a record and CDS is above 1,000bp.
- **High-yield spreads:** stays **watch** at 3.40% (FRED, Oct 22), up from 3.23%.
- **10-year Treasury:** stays **watch** at 4.96%, down from 5.10%.

The bottleneck stays **energy**. HBM and TSMC 3nm are a growing secondary squeeze.

## Exit rules tripped
- **Infrastructure, "Neocloud refinancing at sharply wider spreads": still a near-trip, not tripped.** Secondary yields set a record, but no new CoreWeave unsecured issue was found this week.
- **Models, "lab misses a round or takes a down round": not tripped.** OpenAI's ask fell, but it is still well above the last closed round.
- No other rule tripped. There were no capex cuts or utility load-forecast cuts. NVIDIA gross margin, GPU rental prices, cloud growth and application NRR were not tested this week. The cloud tests come with earnings on Oct 28–29.

## Contested this week
- **CoreWeave CDS:** about 881bp (Dow Jones, Oct 12), above 1,000bp (Reuters via Roic, Oct 14–15) and 1,115bp (Benzinga, Oct 22). The level is rising, and no outlet names a data provider.
- **Anthropic draft S-1:** H1 revenue $16.3B, net loss $80.7B, compute commitments up to $750B, and a $1.5–2T valuation sought. All of it traces back to the WSJ and CNBC, and nothing has been filed publicly yet.
- **OpenAI round:** about $1.4T (Bloomberg, Sep 29) vs $1.25T (CNBC, Oct 22). These are talks, not a closed round.
- **AI data center bonds below par:** the FT (Oct 19), known only through a summary.
- **2027 HBM shortfall:** UBS, single source.
- **Not verified and left out:** BDC discounts to NAV, new data center M&A (the deals found were from June and July), and any model API price changes.
